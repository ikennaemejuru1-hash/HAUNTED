# HAUNTED: Asset Brief

This brief tells you exactly which images to create with ChatGPT image generation, how to word each prompt, and how to hand them over. Kiro then processes them (crop, resize, colour check, WebP and AVIF) and wires them into the site.

Spec references: requirements R15, N4, N9 and design section 8 "Imagery plan" and section 11.

---

## 1. What you create and what Kiro builds in code

| You create with ChatGPT (2D images) | Kiro builds in code (do not generate these) |
|---|---|
| Threshold poster (desktop and mobile) | Fog and mist |
| Hall backplate | Embers, dust motes, smoke |
| Doorway backplate | Candle flames and candlelight |
| Four identity portraits | Gate, arches, pillars (3D geometry) |
| Four tileable material textures | The four 3D artefacts (mirror, grimoire, mask, lantern) |
| Social preview image | Sigils, icons, logo, all text |
| Costume illustrations (later, Phase 4) | Lighting, bloom, vignette, grain |

Generate the **Threshold poster first**. Kiro will match the layout of the 3D scene (gate position, braziers, moon) to your poster, so the poster and the live 3D scene feel like the same place. The poster also appears on slow devices, under reduced motion, and while the 3D loads.

## 2. Rules for every image

1. **Palette only.** Obsidian black #0A0908, charcoal #1E1B1C, deep burgundy #641B35, antique gold #C5A15B, burnt orange #E87524, warm grey #8A8786, warm silver #CFCBC6.
2. **No purple, violet, blue, teal or cyan anywhere.** This includes moonlight, shadows, sky, fog and reflections. Image generators often add cool blue moonlight. Reject any image that has it.
3. **No text.** No letters, numbers, runes that look like real writing, logos, watermarks, signatures or frames.
4. **No real people.** No celebrities, no recognisable faces. The portraits use masks, hoods or deep shadow.
5. **Original designs only.** Nothing that resembles a film, game, comic, brand or existing character.
6. **Not graphic.** Eerie and dramatic, never gory.
7. **Rights.** Check OpenAI's current Terms of Use before submission to confirm you may publish the images. The About page will credit them as "Original artwork generated by the project owner with ChatGPT image generation."

## 3. Style lock (paste this at the start of every prompt)

Copy this block exactly, then add the asset prompt after it. Using the same block every time keeps all images in one consistent style.

```
STYLE LOCK FOR "HAUNTED": Dark cinematic gothic matte painting, premium film still,
moody and elegant, Halloween atmosphere. Painterly but detailed, soft volumetric fog,
deep shadows, warm light sources only (candles, braziers, embers, a warm gold moon).
STRICT PALETTE: obsidian black #0A0908, charcoal #1E1B1C, deep burgundy #641B35,
antique gold #C5A15B, burnt orange #E87524, warm grey #8A8786, warm silver #CFCBC6.
Black and charcoal dominate the image. Gold and burnt orange appear only as light
and highlights. ABSOLUTELY NO purple, violet, blue, teal or cyan anywhere, including
the sky, moonlight, shadows, fog and reflections: all shadows are warm charcoal and
all moonlight is warm amber-gold. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks,
signatures or borders. No real or recognisable people. Original design that does not
resemble any existing film, game, brand or character. Not gory.
```

## 5. Prompts

For each one, paste the **style lock** first, then the prompt.

### A1 Threshold poster, desktop (3:2 landscape)

```
Wide landscape, 3:2. A towering gothic gate stands at the end of a short stone path,
seen from a low camera at night. The gate is a pointed arch of aged dark stone with
antique gold ironwork and tracery, its two doors slightly open with a thin line of
burnt-orange candlelight glowing from inside. Two stone braziers flank the path, their
flames burnt orange, sending small embers drifting upward. A large warm gold moon glows
softly behind and above the gate, half hidden in mist. Layers of low fog roll across
the ground and between the pillars, thicker in the foreground. Bare, twisted trees
fade into the fog at the edges. Deep burgundy tint in the shadows.
COMPOSITION: the gate sits in the lower-centre of the frame. Keep the centre and upper
half of the frame calm, dark and misty with no strong detail, because large text will
be placed there. Edges fall off into near-black vignette.
```

### A2 Threshold poster, mobile (2:3 portrait)

```
Tall portrait, 2:3. The same gothic gate as a cinematic night scene: a towering pointed
arch of aged dark stone with antique gold ironwork, doors slightly open with burnt-orange
candlelight inside, two braziers with rising embers, a warm gold moon behind the gate
in mist, thick layered ground fog, bare twisted trees fading at the sides, burgundy-
tinted shadows.
COMPOSITION: the gate occupies the bottom 45% of the frame, centred. The top 55% is calm
dark misty sky and moon glow with no strong detail, because text will sit there. Edges
fall off into near-black vignette.
```

If you made A1 first in the same chat, add: "Match the gate, braziers and lighting of the previous image."

### A3 Hall backplate (3:2 landscape)

```
Wide landscape, 3:2. Inside a vast abandoned gothic hall at night, seen down its length.
Tall pointed arches and stone columns recede into darkness. Hundreds of candles on iron
stands and on the floor glow burnt orange and gold, their light pooling on worn stone.
Thin mist hangs at floor level. At the far end, a tall doorway glows softly with
warm orange light. Burgundy velvet drapes hang torn from the arches. No figures.
COMPOSITION: symmetrical, vanishing point in the centre. The middle third of the frame
is open floor and soft light (3D objects will float there). Strong falloff into darkness
at the edges and top.
```

### B portraits: shared framing (add this after the style lock for each portrait)

```
Half-length character portrait, 2:3 portrait. One original costumed figure, centred,
body turned three-quarters, face hidden by a mask, hood or deep shadow so no real face
is visible. Pure dark obsidian background with soft warm rim light from behind and a
gentle key light from above. Leave a little empty space above the head. Fashion
editorial quality, the costume is the hero. The figure looks like a Halloween costume
a person could recreate at home, elevated and cinematic.
```

Make all four portraits in the **same chat**, and for the second, third and fourth add: "Same style, lighting, framing and background as the previous portrait."

## 6. If an image is wrong

Reply in the same ChatGPT chat with one of these:

- Blue or purple appeared: `Regenerate with the same composition, but remove every trace of blue and purple. Make the moonlight warm amber-gold and the shadows warm charcoal and burgundy.`
- Text or symbols that look like writing: `Same image, but remove all text, letters and writing-like symbols.`
- Too busy where text goes: `Same image, but make the upper-centre area calmer and darker with only soft mist.`
- Too bright overall: `Same image, darker overall: black and charcoal should dominate, with light only from the flames and moon.`
- Portrait face visible: `Same image, but hide the face completely with the mask, hood or shadow.`

## 7. Checklist before you upload

- [ ] No purple, violet, blue, teal or cyan anywhere (look at the sky, shadows and fog closely).
- [ ] No text, letters, logos, watermarks or signatures.
- [ ] No recognisable face.
- [ ] Composition leaves the calm area where text will sit.
- [ ] Downloaded from ChatGPT as the original file (PNG or WebP). Not a screenshot, not converted to JPEG, not upscaled by another app.
- [ ] Renamed to the exact file name in section 4.

Kiro also runs an automatic colour scan. An image is rejected if more than 0.5% of its pixels fall in the blue-to-purple hue range (190° to 290°) with noticeable saturation. You will get the result and, if needed, a suggested fix.

## 8. How to hand them over

1. Open the repository on GitHub: <https://github.com/ikennaemejuru1-hash/HAUNTED/tree/phase-2-foundation>
2. Make sure the branch selector shows **phase-2-foundation**.
3. Click **Add file → Upload files**.
4. Drag in your images. Before committing, add `assets-src/` to the start of each file name in the path box, for example `assets-src/threshold-poster-desktop.png`. GitHub creates the folder.
5. Choose **Commit directly to the phase-2-foundation branch** and commit.
6. Tell Kiro which files you uploaded.

Upload them in batches (A first) so work can continue while you make the rest.

## 9. What Kiro does with them

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Colour scan | Rejects blue or purple regions (section 7). |
| Crop and resize | To the exact aspect ratio, at several widths for different screens. |
| Colour grade | A light warm grade so every image sits in the same palette. |
| Convert | AVIF and WebP, with the original PNG kept only in `assets-src/`. |
| Budgets | Desktop poster under 250 KB, mobile poster under 150 KB, portraits under 120 KB, textures under 150 KB, costume images under 150 KB. |
| Wire in | Paths are added to `src/data/assets.ts`. Placeholders are swapped with no other code change. |
| Alt text | Portraits get descriptive alt text. Posters and textures are decorative (`alt=""`). |
| Credit | Added to the About page. |
